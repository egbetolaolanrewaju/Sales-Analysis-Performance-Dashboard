# Sales Performance & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance & Customer Analytics Dashboard** developed with **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms sales data into actionable business insights by analyzing revenue, total costs, profit, customer trends, product performance, brand profitability, customer income levels, and purchasing behavior.

The project demonstrates my ability to transform raw business data into clear, interactive, and decision-ready visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- What is the overall revenue, cost, and profit performance?
- Which brands generate the highest profit?
- How does profit vary across customer income levels?
- How does the number of customers change over time?
- How does yearly revenue perform?
- What product categories are included in the sales portfolio?
- How are purchases distributed by product color?
- Which customer segments contribute most to profitability?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Brand Profitability
Analyzes profit generated by major brands, including:

- Apple
- Lenovo
- Samsung
- HP
- Asus
- Dell

### Income Level Analysis
Compares profit contribution across:

- Low-income customers
- Medium-income customers
- High-income customers

### Customer Trend Analysis
Tracks monthly customer activity to identify changes in customer volume over time.

### Yearly Revenue Analysis
Visualizes revenue performance across the available months/periods.

### Purchase Color Analysis
Shows the distribution of total purchases across:

- White
- Gray
- Black

### Interactive Filtering

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Product Name
- Gender

This enables users to perform focused analysis of different customer and product segments.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some observations from the dashboard include:

- Apple generated the highest profit among the brands shown.
- Medium-income customers contributed the highest profit compared with the other income segments.
- Customer activity fluctuated across the displayed months.
- Revenue varied across the reporting periods, with the final period showing the strongest revenue performance.
- White products accounted for the largest share of total purchases among the displayed color categories.
